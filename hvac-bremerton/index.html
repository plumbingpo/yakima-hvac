Keeping Your Bremerton HVAC System Running Longer

The best way to avoid a mid-season breakdown is routine maintenance. We recommend that homeowners replace filters every one to three months, keep shrubs and grass trimmed two feet back from the outdoor condenser, rinse the condenser coil each spring, and have a professional tune-up twice a year, cooling in spring and heating in fall, so small problems get caught while they're still cheap.

Age matters too. Most central air conditioners last 12 to 15 years, furnaces 15 to 20, and heat pumps 10 to 15 with regular service. If your system is past that window and showing symptoms, such as rising energy bills, rooms that never feel comfortable, or repairs stacking up season after season, a planned replacement is nearly always less expensive and far less stressful than an emergency one during the first heat wave of summer.

Minor issues don't stay minor for long. Take a weak capacitor: replacing it is a short, inexpensive repair, but left alone for a season, it can take the compressor with it, and just like that a minor fix has become a full system replacement. That's why we ask homeowners to save our number in their phone and call at the first hint of trouble. Catching it early saves time and money and prevents a miserable night.
