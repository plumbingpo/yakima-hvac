Why Yakima Homeowners Choose Us

Your home is your biggest investment, and a failing HVAC system chips away at your family's comfort every day it goes unaddressed. Left alone, a cracked heat exchanger or a clogged condensate drain can turn a comfort complaint into a safety issue or a water-damage repair. When something goes wrong, you need a team that responds quickly, diagnoses the fault accurately, and fixes it right the first time. That's the standard Yakima HVAC holds itself to.

Our trucks carry the usual failure parts, including capacitors, contactors, igniters, and fan motors, so most routine repairs wrap up on the first visit, whether the system is a gas furnace, a split AC, a heat pump, or a ductless mini-split.

No Surprises

Honest, Upfront Pricing

Before we touch a tool, we find out exactly what's wrong. Our technicians check the age and tonnage of your system, the refrigerant charge, the heat exchanger and coils, and the condition of your ductwork. After that you get a straightforward recommendation and the complete price up front, whether the job is swapping a worn capacitor or replacing the whole system. No hidden fees, no surprise add-ons.

The problem with HVAC failures is that many of them develop where you can't see them. A pinhole leak in the evaporator coil bleeds off refrigerant, the blower bearings wear down gradually, and a weak capacitor lasts right up until the first hot afternoon, when the compressor won't start. When bills climb, airflow drops, or ice forms on the lines, get it looked at while it's still a simple repair.

Do you know how old your system is? Once an air conditioner or furnace passes the 12-year mark, it's worth thinking about the next one before the current one forces the issue. Emergency replacements happen on the worst days, in heat waves and cold snaps, when options are few and everyone is uncomfortable. Our team knows how the Yakima climate affects equipment and will tell you plainly whether yours has years left or is nearing the end.

Seasonal Tune-Ups

Tune-Ups That Keep Your System Running

Routine maintenance is the most reliable way to keep your home comfortable year-round. Regular service prevents failures before they start, keeps coils and burners clean, and catches small faults while they're still cheap to fix. At Yakima HVAC, every plan is built around your home's actual equipment, with scheduled visits that cover coil cleaning, refrigerant charge verification, capacitor and contactor testing, burner and heat exchanger inspection, condensate drain clearing, filter replacement, and a static pressure check across the duct system.

Many homes do best with two visits a year: a springtime tune-up for cooling and a fall tune-up for heating. The schedule flexes with the age of your equipment, how hard your household works it, and local conditions such as dust and pollen, which coat condenser coils and push up energy use fast.

Consider maintenance as a safeguard for equipment you've already paid for. Each tune-up helps keep the coils clean, the charge correct, and the filter fresh, and that lowers monthly energy bills while easing the strain on the compressor and blower. For most families, the plan costs far less than an early replacement.

Proper Sizing

The Right Size System for Your Home

Getting the size right matters more than most homeowners realize. Before recommending new equipment, Yakima HVAC measures the details that determine your home's heating and cooling load: square footage, insulation, window exposure, and duct layout. That gives you a system that runs in long, efficient cycles, keeps humidity in check, and keeps every room at the temperature you set.

Choosing new equipment is about more than efficiency ratings. Our technician will compare heat pumps against a furnace-and-AC pair, and ducted against ductless, laying out operating costs, electrical and venting requirements, and the installation itself. Growing families frequently do best with two-stage or variable-speed equipment, which holds more even temperatures from room to room and uses less energy. You choose what fits your home and budget.

Repairs We Handle

Common HVAC Problems We Fix in Yakima

Warm air from the vents, a furnace that refuses to light, ice on the indoor coil, leaking refrigerant: every one of these problems has its own cause and needs a different repair. Yakima HVAC diagnoses the exact fault before swapping any parts, whether it's a condenser that hums without starting, a short-cycling furnace, a thermostat that loses its settings, a clogged condensate drain, or a mini-split throwing error codes.

Not every HVAC problem is equally urgent. A bad capacitor only keeps the compressor from starting. A cracked heat exchanger, on the other hand, can let combustion gases into the house, and a backed-up condensate drain can quietly ruin a ceiling before anyone notices. Our technicians carry the parts to swap capacitors, contactors, fan motors, igniters, flame sensors, and control boards, seal ductwork that's leaking air into the attic, and recharge refrigerant once a leak is fixed.
