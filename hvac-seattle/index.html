Heating and Air Conditioning Service Across Seattle

Heating and cooling around here means a wide mix of equipment: aging furnaces on the older streets, newer heat pumps in recent construction, ductless mini-splits in additions and converted garages, and rooftop units on commercial buildings. Our team handles all of it. Our technicians have years in the field, and we've learned how the local climate, the housing stock, and local code affect the way a system performs and fails.

Most homeowners we work with need one of three things: a repair, a replacement, or regular maintenance. Repairs are usually straightforward: a failed capacitor, a dirty flame sensor, a slow refrigerant leak, or a worn-out blower motor. Replacements get a proper load calculation, not a guess based on the old unit's tonnage, which keeps temperatures even and stops the new equipment from short-cycling its way to an early failure. Tune-ups include coil cleaning, a refrigerant charge check, safety control testing, and a static pressure reading, which does more than anything else to extend the life of your system.

For commercial property owners and managers nearby, we service the rooftop units, split systems, and makeup air and ventilation equipment that restaurants, retail spaces, offices, and multi-unit buildings depend on. We understand that a hot dining room or a freezing office means lost business, so we prioritize fast diagnosis, carry common parts on the truck, and schedule work around your operating hours whenever possible.

Quick Heating and Cooling Help in Seattle

When your system quits, how quickly help shows up matters more than anything else. For that reason, we keep scheduling flexible across the area and answer every call with a live person. Emergency calls usually get a technician within a day, and we prioritize a failed furnace in a cold snap or a dead AC in the heat as top priority.

Common emergencies we handle include air conditioners blowing warm air, outdoor units that hum but won't start, furnaces that ignite and then shut down, breakers that trip whenever the system starts, frozen evaporator coils, clogged condensate drains backing up into the furnace cabinet, and thermostats that have simply given up. Our trucks carry the capacitors, contactors, igniters, flame sensors, universal control boards, and fittings needed to complete most repairs in a single visit. We provide upfront pricing every time, so you know exactly what the work will cost before we begin.

Two situations call for a quick step from you before a technician arrives. The first is an iced-over air conditioner: switch the system off at the thermostat and set the fan to "on" so the moving air thaws the coil, because running it frozen can overflow the drain pan and hurt the compressor. The second is a furnace that short-cycles or gives off a burning smell. Shut it down at the switch and call us. Those few minutes can save you from a far bigger repair.

How Seattle Homeowners Can Extend HVAC Life

Many mid-season breakdowns start with skipped maintenance, and the habits that prevent them are easy. Change your filter every one to three months, keep plants and shrubs at least two feet from the outdoor condenser, and hose off the condenser coil once a year in spring. Then book a professional tune-up twice a year, one for cooling and one for heating, so our technicians can catch small problems while they're still cheap to fix.

Age matters too. Most central air conditioners run 12 to 15 years, furnaces 15 to 20, and heat pumps 10 to 15 with regular service. If your system is past that window and starting to struggle, such as rising energy bills, rooms that never feel comfortable, or repairs stacking up season after season, a planned replacement is nearly always less expensive and far less stressful than an emergency one in the middle of a cold snap.

If comfort problems do show up, acting quickly keeps them small. A weak capacitor is a quick fix. The same capacitor left alone for a season can take the compressor with it and turn a minor repair into a full system replacement. It's smart to keep our number saved on your phone so you can reach us at the first sign of trouble. Early intervention saves time, money, and miserable nights.
